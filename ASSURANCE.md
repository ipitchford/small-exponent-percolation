# Assurance and semantic boundaries

The main theorem concerns independent **undirected** Bernoulli bonds of probability pD(x), not 1-exp(-pD(x)). The normalized parameter p is expected degree. The correction is the two-step return weight sum D(x)^2.

The proof imports HHS's kernel-general infrared theorem and established first-coefficient event identities. Its refinement keeps the spatial supremum diagram of order sigma distinct from closed diagrams of order sigma squared, and retains two event restrictions. A positive odd-truncation remainder then supplies an upper threshold bound entirely through subcritical parameters. This does not mean the imported infrared theorem avoids higher-order lace estimates.

The generalization requires maximum kernel weight O(e), J_(2,3)=O(e^2), and J_(3,2)=O(e^3), with symmetry, zero diagonal and the structural/model hypotheses stated in the manuscript. Small maximum weight alone is insufficient. Applications include positive summable perturbations and mixtures with exponent ratios in a fixed compact positive interval. Constants may depend on the prescribed bounds defining those classes.

Finite diagnostics below check exact rational algebra and normalization. They cannot establish the event inclusions, HHS hypotheses for arbitrary kernels, or uniform analytic remainder by sampling. The analytic manuscript remains the proof object. There is no explicit useful remainder constant or moderate-sigma validity interval, no third-order coefficient and no claimed universal result for other edge laws.
