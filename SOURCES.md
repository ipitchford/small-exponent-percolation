# Load-bearing sources and comparison scope

- Heydenreich, van der Hofstad and Sakai (2008), *Mean-field behavior for long- and finite range Ising model, percolation and self-avoiding walk*. Assumption 2.1, Theorem 2.3, equation (2.6), and weighted infinite-volume identity supply the general input. https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.0312 ; DOI 10.1007/s10955-008-9580-5.
- van der Hofstad and Sakai (2005), *Critical points for spread-out self-avoiding walk, percolation and the contact process above the upper critical dimensions*. Theorem 1.1 equation (1.14) already contains the return correction; Section 3.2 contains the inherited first-coefficient decomposition. https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0402050 ; DOI 10.1007/s00440-004-0405-4.
- Borgs, Chayes, van der Hofstad, Slade and Spencer (2005), *Random subgraphs of finite graphs. II. The lace expansion and the triangle condition*. Event definitions and positive finite-order remainder are cited precisely in the paper. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401070 ; DOI 10.1214/009117905000000260.
- Amit (2026), *Explicit upper bounds on the threshold of one-dimensional long-range percolation*, arXiv:2610.11872v1. Motivating undirected small-exponent question and monotone-path bounds; not the correction proof here. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11872v1.
- Liu (2025), *High-dimensional long-range statistical mechanical models have random walk correlation functions*, arXiv:2502.12104. The supplied review inspected version 3, Appendix A, for a different continuity-avoidance argument; attempted version-2 routes failed. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12104.

No third-party full text is bundled or relicensed. Precise references and versions in the manuscript take precedence over abbreviated descriptions here.
